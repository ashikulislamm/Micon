#### CONSTRUCTION OF ETP, WTP, STP, AND RMS ROOMS

Construction of ETP, WTP, STP, and RMS Rooms refers to the development of specialized civil and structural facilities designed to house and support **Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP)**, **Water Treatment Plants (WTP)**, **Sewage Treatment** **Plants (STP)**, and **Regulating & Metering Stations (RMS)**.

These structures are engineered with high precision to ensure durability, chemical resistance, and operational safety, enabling smooth functioning of treatment and gas regulation systems in industrial and commercial projects.
